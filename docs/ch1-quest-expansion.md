# Chapter 1 quest expansion: a 2–4 hour *Sealed Zone* (proposal)

**Status:** proposal for the owner to approve. The approved main act order stays exactly as it is. Everything below is **added around it**: side quests, a longer temple escape, and exploration that fills the empty map (see [ch1-fill-plan.png](ch1-fill-plan.png)). Names are placeholders. All of it follows the rules in [ch1-script.md](ch1-script.md).

## Time budget (a first-time player)

| Part | Now | With the expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue + temple escape | ~5 min | **20–25 min** (the temple is 1.4x and gets a real escape) |
| Main acts I–V + finale (bosses, camps, travel) | ~30–40 min | **60–80 min** (bigger packs, more travel, boss phases) |
| Side quests (11 below) | 0 | **70–110 min** |
| Exploration: 6 fragments, 5 chests, 5 shrines, vistas | ~0 | **20–30 min** |
| **Total** | **~40 min** | **~2.5–4 h** |

## 1. The main path, deepened (no order changes)

**Prologue+ — *The Sealed Halls* (temple escape).** He wakes at the broken pillar, but the doors out are still sealed. He crosses the bigger temple through three short trials, each teaching one skill, and **lights three seal lamps** to open the Lotus Court:
- **the Canal Gallery:** jumping and the stand-on-ledges rules (broken walkways over the canal)
- **the Shrine:** combat (a ghoul pack)
- **the Offering Hall stairs:** dash (a collapsing stair)

Then the Stone Warden as now. *Lore: the prison was built to hold him inside the temple first.*

**Acts I–V:** as approved. Each act gets **one more step before its boss**, so the region is played, not just passed:

| Act | New step | Why (lore) |
|---|---|---|
| I-b Undead King | Climb the Offering Hall terrace from the **outside**, through a ruined gallery; his chains are bolted in 3 places, and breaking them wakes him | he is *bound* to the temple as the jailer |
| II Goblin Chief | Ma Hnin is moved between camps: track her by the goblins' **drag marks** (she "reads the land" later; here he does it alone) | the goblins capture and move captives |
| III Necromancer | Break **3 bone obelisks** that keep raising the dead; then he comes out to fight | the Necromancer raises the captives |
| IV Commander | Cross the **Garrison Trench** under archer fire, open the gate winch | soldiers patrol every road out |
| V Weeping Maid | Follow her crying through the fog of **Lantern Bog** (she moves; the lanterns show the way) | the marsh holds the spirits |
| Finale | Before the lair: **Naga Hollow**, where the shed skin and an egg carry the same red seal-brand as the Naga | it is a prisoner too, which sets up the mercy |

## Elements in the quests (the spell system)
The seal tore Zaw Gyi's alchemy apart and bound each element to a jailer (see ch1-script.md). The main acts **free** the elements (Fire at the start, Earth from the Undead King, Air from the Goblin Chief, Water from the Necromancer, Spirit from the Weeping Maid). The side quests **master** them:
- **Element Mastery** (5, one per side quest above): every spell that uses that element hits about 20% harder or lasts longer, and its passive while circling doubles.
- **Element puzzles** in the world, so the elements matter outside fights:
  - **🔥** burns bramble walls (shortcuts)
  - **🪨 Stone Bulwark** makes a step to a high ledge, or dams water
  - **🌪** clears poison fog
  - **💧** fills dry offering bowls (opens shrines)
  - **🦋 Butterfly Ascent** reaches the highest ledges (the watchtower, the stupa top)
- **Prologue+ (The Sealed Halls)** teaches the system with Fire alone: Fire+Fire (Alchemy Blast) opens the first seal lamp.

## 3. Collectibles and travel
- **6 Lost Fragments** (lines of the childhood memory): 3 from the Drowned Monastery, 1 from the hairpin, 1 atop the Crossroads Knoll, 1 at the bottom of the Naga's Scar.
- **5 opening chests:** the Knoll, the Watchtower, the Garrison Trench floor, the Empty Village, the Snare Fields.
- **5 Soul Shrines:** activate one by touching it; then fast travel between them, and they refill health and mana.
