# Story bible and Chapter 1 script (working draft v3)

All names are placeholders and can be renamed later.

## The whole game in one page

**Before the game.** Zaw Gyi, an alchemist (*zawgyi*), was a righteous saint who gave the land hope. The people loved him more than their king.
- **His master:** the **Old Wizard**, the king's **most trusted advisor**, who led the land into its **golden age**. Zaw Gyi was his apprentice and heir.
- **The rivals:** the **Council of Nine Witches** were **the Old Wizard's rivals** at court. They spent years losing to him, waiting for the day he was gone. When he finally **grew tired of court and left**, they made their move.
- **The whisper:** they told the king that a man the people worship is a crown waiting to be taken.

**The sealing.** The king and the witches drugged Zaw Gyi and sealed him in the old jungle temple.
- **Why seal, not kill:** sealing **erases him from every memory in the land.** The people forgot their saint, and came to remember the **witches** as their saints instead.

**The princess.** She overheard her father and the witches plotting and went to the temple alone and in secret.
- **The crack:** all she could do was press her blood into the ritual. **Royal blood** sealed him, so royal blood could crack the seal.
- **The cost:** she turned to stone while watching her friend slowly being sealed.
- **Her memory survived:** she was already stone when the erasure took everyone's memory. The world forgot she existed too.
- **The witness:** her **trusted maid** saw her slip away and followed her, and watched it happen.

**The witches take over.** Right after the sealing they **killed the king** and bound his corpse to the temple as Zaw Gyi's jailer.
- **The fake king:** their **leader (Witch 1)** took the king's form and has ruled ever since.
- **The statue:** the witches carried the princess's statue to the palace. They want her royal blood and hope she'll change back.

**How they last.** Each witch has a land assigned to her and **drains its life force** to stay young, like the witch in Snow White. That's why the land is dying and evil creatures roam everywhere.

**Five hundred years later**, the princess's flaw in the seal has finally spread and **the seal cracks**. Zaw Gyi wakes, and this is his **revenge arc**.

**The present-day ally.** **Ma Hnin**, a chosen girl from a hidden village, dreams of a stranger's resurrection without knowing who he is. She becomes his first ally and guide.

## Chapter 1 — *The Sealed Zone*

The jungle around the temple is the witches' prison zone. It has no witch, only the jailers they bound there 500 years ago. The creatures are the seal's guards, and the land is choked and dying.

**The design rule for this chapter:** *every major encounter changes what Zaw Gyi understands.* The fights build one story (*"Can I escape?"*), not separate camps on the way to a dragon. The chapter answers that question, and opens a bigger one: *"Who saved me?"*

### How the prison works: each region's job

The regions are parts of one machine, so the prison feels connected:

| Region | What the jailers do there | What it shows Zaw Gyi |
|---|---|---|
| **Goblin Hunting Grounds** | The goblins **capture** anyone who wanders near the temple. | The prison still feeds on people. |
| **Bone Wastes** | The Necromancer **raises the captives** as the dead army. | Where the captured go. |
| **Undead Garrison** | Soldiers **patrol every road out** of the valley. | It was built to keep *him* in. |
| **Ghost Marsh** | It **holds the spirits** of those who died here, unable to leave. | Even death doesn't escape this place. |
| **Naga's Lair** | The mind-bound Naga **guards the only gate**. | The lock itself. |

### Ma Hnin's role (more than the altar)

She knows the **present** and he remembers fragments of the **past**. Their short exchanges after each act show how much has changed:
- he names a city she has only heard of as ruins
- she prays to the **"Nine Saints"**, the witches the land now worships, and he goes cold
- she has never heard his name, yet she dreamed of his face

She reads the land for him: the tracks, which camps hold captives, where the marsh lights are. She still runs the altar too.

## Rules to keep consistent (no plot holes)

1. **Nobody in the present remembers Zaw Gyi**, except in dreams (Ma Hnin) or as feelings the curse left behind (the Banshee).
2. **Nobody remembers the princess at all**, except the witches, who know.
3. **The first time "she" is mentioned** is the Necromancer's line in Act III, and no name is given in Chapter 1.
4. **Zaw Gyi remembers the betrayal** (the king, the chanting) but **not the princess's sacrifice**. He was being sealed and never saw who cracked it.
5. **The witches never appear in Chapter 1** except as hooded figures in memories, and the fake king in the epilogue shot.
6. **The undead king is truly the real king.** The fake king in the palace is Witch 1.
